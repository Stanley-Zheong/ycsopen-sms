# Phase 14 Spec

## Goal

Only authorized bounded exemptions can become effective.

## Scope

- OBL-F-3-6-A: an operator can create signature, content, or account exemptions with tenant, resource, product, scope, approval state, reason, and validity.
- OBL-F-3-6-B: policy preview applies deterministic precedence for expired, revoked, overlapping, out-of-scope, unauthorized, and non-exemptable controls.
- OBL-F-3-6-C: every exemption decision and policy use records exemption version, subject, actor, reason, and result.

## Out of scope

- General resource review history.
- Runtime sensitive-word authoring.
- Third-party blacklist/risk provider controls.
